About page

This is a website developed by Tom Foord for MedCity

The map shows where clinical trials are being run across the UK and which organisations are involved. Each circle is an organisation, size relative to the number of studies it is linked to. Click a circle to see the list of trials, with links to their ISRCTN registry entries. Last updated in 2022.

Use the Diseases/Interventions menu to filter by condition category or intervention type.

Data

Trial data come from the ISRCTN registry. The data are a snapshot from early 2022 and have not been updated since.

Update from author 2026:

I made this website while studying at medical school during the COVID-19 pandemic. It is simple in concept, but taught me a lot about how to build website infrastructure and visualise data in a novel way. These days it could probably be made in an afternoon using a generative AI tool, but I am proud of it all the same.

The map was originally hosted here on the Medcity website, but has since become deprecated with updates. I am hosting it here for now, but the website remains property of Medcity.
